About Our Campus

Ghana Communication Technology University (GCTU) is one of Ghana's leading institutions specializing in Information and Communication Technology. Located in Accra, the university provides quality education and a modern learning environment for students from Ghana and other countries.

Our mission is to empower students with knowledge and practical skills. Programmes include Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, and Business Administration.

Founded in 2005. Laboratories support scientific studies such as H2O analysis and experiments conducted at 37°C*.

Important: Admissions are open for prospective students.
